# Industry cost and conversion benchmarks: SMB SaaS funnels and TikTok/Meta lead generation (researched 12/11/2026)

Conversions use $1 ≈ 26,000 VND. Hubsell inputs from the brief: about 255 signups, about 153 connected a shop, 16 paying (about 6% signup→paid). ARPU is about 250k VND/month (≈ $9.6). The 14-day trial needs no card. TikTok Traffic: CPC about 458 VND, CTR 3.2%, 0 signups from 423 clicks. TikTok DM lead gen: CPM about 26k VND, about 50k VND per conversation, 200k VND per lead (4 conversations, 1 lead). One organic founder video brought in about 100 signups.

## 1. SMB SaaS funnel benchmarks (visitor→signup, trial→paid, freemium→paid)

### Takeaway
The best recent primary dataset is ChartMogul / Growth Unhinged / ProductLed (survey of 200 products, January 2026). For a no-card free trial, the median ("good") conversion to paid within 6 months is 4–6%, and the top quartile ("great") is 10–15%. Hubsell's about 6% signup→paid is therefore normal for a no-card trial, at the median, not weak. First Page Sage reports a higher figure (about 18% for no-card trials), but its definition and sample differ (see conflicts below).

### Cited Findings
- **ChartMogul × Growth Unhinged (Kyle Poyar) × ProductLed, "The Conversion Report"**
  - Published 04/02/2026, survey January 2026, 200 responses.
  - Conversion means a free signup who pays within 6 months.
  - Typical respondent: $1–10M ARR and ARPA $50–249/month. 21% of respondents have ARPA under $50/month.
  - No-card free trial: good 4–6%, great 10–15%. No-card trials are 80% of free-trial products.
  - Card-required trial: good 25–35%, great 50–60%.
  - Gated freemium: 3–5% / 8–12%. Ungated freemium: 7–9% / 8–12%. Reverse trial: 4–6% / 8–12% (small sample).
  - Overall median is 8%. The distribution is bimodal: for free trials, 20% of products are below 2.5% and 23% are above 25%. The top 20% and bottom 20% of self-serve products differ by about 10x.
  - By segment (good/great): SaaS 5–7% / 12–16%, B2B 6–10% / 15–20%.
  - Per 1,000 website visitors: all free trials give a 4.5% visitor→signup rate (45 signups) and 8% to paid (3.6 customers). Card-required trials give 3.5% (35 signups) and 30% to paid (10.5 customers). All freemium gives 9% (90 signups) and about 5.5% to paid (5 customers).
  - 62% of trials last 14 days.
  - The report does not break conversion out by ARPA or price band.
  - [ChartMogul – The Conversion Report](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/)
- **First Page Sage** (data Q1 2022 – Q3 2025, 86 SaaS clients of this agency, 71% B2B; updated 05/09/2025). "Conversion" here means paying at least 1 month.

  | Funnel step | Organic | Paid traffic |
  |---|---|---|
  | Visitor → no-card trial | 8.5% | 7.1% |
  | No-card trial → paid | 18.2% | 17.4% |
  | Visitor → card-required trial | 2.5% | 2.2% |
  | Card-required trial → paid | 48.8% | 51% |
  | Visitor → freemium | 13.3% | 15.9% |
  | Freemium → paid | 2.6% | 2.8% |

  - Advertising/AdTech industry: visitor→trial 9.1%, trial→paid 24.3%.
  - [First Page Sage – SaaS Free Trial Conversion Rate Benchmarks](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-free-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks/)
- **Userpilot** summarizes both sources above. It also cites an undated ProductLed survey (600+ B2B SaaS companies): free→paid is about 9% across all models, and about 25% for teams that use PQLs (Product Qualified Leads). Activation benchmark: Userpilot gives no general figure, only a Rocketbots case where activation rose from 15% to 30% after adding a walkthrough. — [Userpilot – Free trial conversion rate](https://userpilot.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate/)
- **Conflict:** several secondary blogs quote 18–25% for no-card trials, attributing it to "OpenView 2024", Flint and others. I could not verify this against OpenView's original report. It conflicts with ChartMogul 2026 (median 4–6%). — [search summary: innertrends, flint, databox in WebSearch results](https://www.innertrends.com/blog/saas-free-trial-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- Hubsell at 16/255 ≈ 6.3% sits at the top of ChartMogul's "good" band for no-card trials, and well below First Page Sage's 18%. Because the two sources define conversion differently (paid within 6 months vs. paid ≥1 month), the closer comparison is ChartMogul: 6% is normal, and the target to aim for is 10–15% (top quartile).
- Shop connection (153/255 = 60%) is the closest equivalent of "activation". Paid / connected = 16/153 ≈ 10.5%, which is near ChartMogul's "great" band. The bottleneck is more likely the step from connected to paid, plus the 40% who sign up but never connect, than the signup step itself.
- Benchmark visitor→signup for no-card trials is about 4.5% (ChartMogul) to 7–8.5% (First Page Sage). This is the yardstick for the TikTok Traffic campaign result (see section 3).

### Gaps
- I found no reliable benchmark that breaks trial conversion out by ARPU under $20/month, or for Vietnam / Southeast Asia.
- I found no trustworthy industry-wide "signup → activation" benchmark, only individual cases.

## 2. CAC payback, LTV:CAC, SMB churn, and maximum affordable CAC at ARPU ≈ $10

### Takeaway
SMB / low-ACV SaaS is usually expected to pay back CAC within about 8–12 months. Monthly logo churn for low-ARPU SMB tools commonly runs 3–7%, and about 6–7% for ARPU ≤ $10. At 250k VND/month ARPU, the maximum affordable CAC (paid customer acquisition cost) is therefore only about 0.7–1.2 million VND per paying customer. That means at most about 40–75k VND per signup at the current 6% conversion.

### Cited Findings
- **CAC payback (vendor sources, not primary reports; treat as directional):**
  - Optifai benchmark of 939 B2B SaaS companies (data Q2 2025 – Q1 2026): SMB with ACV under $15K pays back in about 8–12 months; mid-market 14–18; enterprise 18–24. Overall B2B median is 15 months. SMB NRR (net revenue retention) median is 97% vs. 118% for enterprise.
  - For ACV under $5K, the median is 9 months according to "Benchmarkit 2024" and 11 months according to another source. These conflict.
  - The "<12 months" rule of thumb for SMB is attributed to David Skok (Matrix Partners).
  - The Caugia GTM Benchmark 2026 reports the 2024 median at 18 months, up from 14 months in 2023.
  - These numbers come from a WebSearch summary of: [SaaS Hero – CAC payback benchmarks](https://saashero.net/customer-retention/cac-payback-period-benchmarks-saas/), [Drivetrain](https://drivetrain.ai/strategic-finance-glossary/cac-payback-period-formula-benchmarks-and-how-to-reduce-it), [GrowthSpree 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/b2b-saas-cac-payback-period-benchmarks-2026-by-stage-vertical-gtm-motion).
- **SMB churn (secondary sources; definitions vary):**
  - ARPU ≤ $10: monthly churn about 6.2%. Another source gives 6.7%, but that is revenue churn.
  - Median SMB monthly logo churn: 4.7% in one compilation; 6.2% in another (small sample, n=38); 2.1–2.5% in a third.
  - Recurly's overall average (all segments) is 3.27%/month.
  - Sources: WebSearch summary of [Vena Solutions](https://venasolutions.com/blog/saas-churn-rate), [UserJot 2025](https://userjot.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks), [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/saas-churn-rate-benchmark), [ProfitWell Churn Benchmarks 2021Q3 PDF](https://www.profitwell.com/hubfs/Gated%20Assets/ProfitWell_Churn_Benchmarks_2021Q3.pdf).
  - I did not open the Recurly / ChartMogul originals to verify the figures by ARPU band.

### Inferences (my own calculations, not from sources)
Assumptions: ARPU 250k VND/month; gross margin assumed at about 80% (Hubsell has not given a real figure); monthly logo churn tested at 4%, 6% and 10%.

| Monthly churn | Average lifetime (months) | Gross-margin LTV (VND) | Max CAC at LTV:CAC = 3 | Max CAC at 12-month payback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4% | 25 | ≈ 5.0M | ≈ 1.67M | ≈ 2.4M |
| 6% | 16.7 | ≈ 3.3M | ≈ 1.1M | ≈ 2.4M |
| 10% | 10 | ≈ 2.0M | ≈ 0.67M | ≈ 2.0M (constrained by lifetime) |

- **LTV:CAC = 3:1 is a common rule of thumb.** I did not fetch a primary source for it this session. With high churn, this constraint is tighter than payback.
- **Realistic CAC ceiling (6% churn): about 1.1M VND per paying customer (≈ $42).**
  - Converted to cost per signup at a 6% signup→paid rate: about 66k VND/signup. At 10% (top quartile): about 110k VND/signup.
  - Converted to cost per connected shop (10.5% connected→paid): about 115k VND.
- **The DM lead campaign at 200k VND/lead** is affordable only if lead→paid ≥ about 18% (1.1M × 0.18 ≈ 200k). That is 3x the current signup→paid rate. It is only plausible if DM leads are much hotter than a normal signup, which has not been proven (only 1 lead).
- The organic founder video (about 100 signups at 0 VND) is consistent with the OpenView data in section 5: organic channels dominate for low-ARPU PLG products.

### Gaps
- I did not reach the Recurly / ChartMogul / Paddle originals for churn by ARPU band in 2024–2026. The figures above are second-hand.
- Hubsell's real churn data is not available yet (paying customers are too new). Measure real logo churn after 2–3 billing cycles, then recompute the table.

## 3. TikTok Ads benchmarks (CPM, CPC, CTR, CVR, CPL; B2B; Vietnam / Southeast Asia)

### Takeaway
I found no authoritative primary source for TikTok CPM/CPL in Vietnam. Every figure comes from agency or vendor blogs and they conflict widely. Global TikTok CPL is about $15–60 for B2B/SaaS (≈ 390k–1.56M VND), so Hubsell's 200k VND/lead is cheap by global standards. Hubsell's CPM (26k VND ≈ $1) and CPC (458 VND ≈ $0.018) are both very low. The real problem with the Traffic campaign is the click→signup conversion (0/423), not the price of the clicks.

### Cited Findings
- **CPL from agency/vendor sources (2025–2026, mostly US/global):**
  - $8–15 for B2C and $25–60 for B2B (SaaS, finance).
  - $15–90, with a median of $18 across industries in 2026.
  - $15–40 for SMB lead forms.
  - Vendor claim: Lead Gen Forms are 30–50% cheaper than landing pages.
  - Sources: WebSearch summary of [LeadGen Economy 2026](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/tiktok-lead-generation-guide/), [Sotros Infotech 2026](https://sotrosinfotech.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-per-lead-benchmarks-by-industry-2026/), [ABE Agency – TikTok B2B](https://www.abetheagency.com/guide/tiktok-industry-benchmarks-for-b2b), [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-cost-benchmarks).
- **Vietnam / Southeast Asia CPM (vendor blogs, no methodology):**
  - Vietnam In-Feed CPM about 30k–80k VND, vs. Meta about 50k–120k VND, according to an undated Storefuel guide.
  - "Tier 3" markets including Southeast Asia: about $1–3. Spark Ads in the region: about $2–4.
  - Global CPM in 2025 about $13.26 (TikTok) vs. $14.19 (Meta), likely US-weighted.
  - Sources: [Storefuel – TikTok Ads ecommerce Vietnam](https://storefuel.pages.dev/vn-en/ecommerce-advertising-guide/tiktok-ads-ecommerce-vietnam/), [Stackmatix – TikTok ads cost by country 2026](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-by-country-2026), [Influee – TikTok CPM 2026](https://influee.co/au/blog/tiktok-cpm).
- **Scale context:** TikTok reports more than 460 million users in Southeast Asia (25/11/2025). Vietnamese outlets cite 70 million in Vietnam. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-surpasses-460-million-users-in-southeast-asia-inks-partnership-with-vietnams-ministry-of-culture-sports-and-tourism); [Tuoi Tre News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/tiktok-tops-460-million-monthly-active-users-in-southeast-asia-with-70-million-in-vietnam-103251029162827365.htm)
- **Meta for comparison** (WordStream/LocaliQ 2025, US accounts managed by LocaliQ): average CPL for lead campaigns $27.66 (up 21% year on year); CPC for traffic campaigns $0.70; CPL by industry ranges from $3.16 (restaurants) to $76.71 (dental). Business services at $16.95 CPL is only a third-party blog figure and unverified. — WebSearch summary of [Search Engine Land – Facebook ad costs jump 21% in 2025](https://searchengineland.com/facebook-ad-costs-jump-beat-google-461690), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/facebook-ad-costs-jump-21-as-lead-campaigns-struggle-while-traffic-ads-thrive/). (The WordStream page returned 403, so I could not read it directly.)

### Inferences
- **Hubsell CPM is below every vendor range.** Hubsell's CPM of 26k VND ≈ $1 is below the Vietnam In-Feed range (30–80k) and at the bottom of the "Tier 3" range ($1–3). Price per impression is not the problem.
- **Traffic campaign, 0 signups from 423 clicks:**
  - At the benchmark visitor→signup rate of 4.5% (ChartMogul), about 19 signups would be expected. Even at a very pessimistic 1%, about 4.2 would be expected, and the chance of seeing 0 would be only about 1.5% (Poisson).
  - So 0/423 is not bad luck. The clicks are genuinely low-intent, as is typical of the TikTok "Traffic" objective (many accidental taps and quick exits), or the landing page / tracking is broken.
  - **Recommendation:** check landing page load time on mobile and whether the TikTok in-app browser drops signups before concluding that the channel fails.
- **The DM lead result (4 conversations / 1 lead) is far too small a sample to conclude anything.** The 95% confidence interval for "1 lead from 4 conversations" spans roughly 1–81%.

### Gaps
- No TikTok official or third-party audited source gives CPL / CVR for B2B SaaS in Vietnam.
- I did not find a reliable TikTok CTR benchmark by industry (only vendor figures), so I cannot say whether a 3.2% CTR is good or bad by source standards.

## 4. Click-to-message / DM ads: cost per conversation and conversation→lead

### Takeaway
Neither Meta nor TikTok publishes an industry benchmark for cost per conversation. Public data consists only of TikTok case studies reporting "percentage reduction" (not absolute VND figures) and vendor blogs. A good relative signal: TikTok cites a Vietnamese case (Funix) with a 6.6% conversion rate on DM ads.

### Cited Findings
- **TikTok messaging-ad case studies in Asia-Pacific (published 10/01/2025):**
  - Vietnam (Phương Đông × Funix, Direct Messaging Ads): 6.6% conversion rate, 20% of engaged users bought in person, cost per conversation down 60%.
  - Indonesia (HydroClean): cost per conversation down 74%.
  - Thailand (Clyn): 22.5% conversion rate, more than 900 conversations in one week, cost per conversation 67% lower than click-based campaigns.
  - Absolute VND/USD figures are not disclosed. These are success stories TikTok selected itself, so they are biased.
  - [Marketing-Interactive – TikTok expands messaging ad solutions in APAC](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/tiktok-expands-messagin-ad-solutions-in-asia-pacific)
- **Meta:** no published benchmark for click-to-message. Vendor CPL/conversation figures for India are ₹50–500 depending on the vertical. Separately, WhatsApp API pricing switched from per-conversation to per-template message in mid-2025, so pre-2025 benchmarks are outdated. — WebSearch summary of [Spur – Click to WhatsApp ads benchmarks](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks), [Kanal 2026](https://getkanal.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks-2026.md), [AdInTime HK 2025](https://adintime.hk/en/blog/whatsapp-advertising-in-hong-kong-all-you-need-to-know-n190)

### Inferences
- 50k VND per conversation ≈ $1.9. There is no reliable benchmark to compare it against.
- The more useful yardstick is the section 2 CAC ceiling:
  - With about 1.1M VND per paying customer, a 50k VND conversation is viable if conversation→paid ≥ about 4.5%.
  - That figure is close to the 6.6% in TikTok's Vietnamese case, which is real but cherry-picked.
- With only 4 conversations, the "1 lead per 4 conversations" ratio (25%) means nothing yet. At least 30–50 conversations are needed to estimate conversation→lead with an error margin of about ±10–15 points.

### Gaps
- No public source gives an absolute cost per conversation for TikTok/Meta in Vietnam, or a conversation → qualified lead rate for B2B SaaS.

## 5. Organic/referral vs. paid share in SMB / PLG SaaS growth

### Takeaway
OpenView data (2021–2023) consistently shows that organic channels (search, word of mouth, referral) bring in most new users for PLG/freemium SaaS. Paid ads account for only about 10–25%, and organic leads convert to paid better than paid leads.

### Cited Findings
- **2022, best freemium companies:** organic search ~53%, product referral ~13%, paid ads ~10%, outbound ~8% of new users.
- **2022, all PLG companies:** organic ~41%, paid ~25%, sales ~12%.
- **2021:** PLG companies got about 50% of new users from organic.
- **2023:** organic was still the top channel but its share fell. I could not confirm the exact figures because the page was truncated.
- **2020 report:** leads from organic (word of mouth, content, social) convert to paid better than leads from paid marketing.
- Sources: WebSearch summary of [OpenView 2022 Product Benchmarks](https://openviewpartners.com/2022-product-benchmarks/), [OpenView – Product Benchmarks 2022 blog](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/product-benchmarks-2022/), [Wing VC – breakdown of OpenView 2023](https://wing.vc/content/openviews-2023-product-benchmarks-report). (OpenView stopped operating in 2024, so there is no newer report.)
- **First Page Sage:** organic and paid traffic perform about the same within each trial model. For example, no-card trial → paid is 18.2% organic vs. 17.4% paid. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-free-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks/)

### Inferences
- About 100 of 255 signups (≈ 40%) came from one organic founder video at 0 VND. That matches the organic-dominant pattern of PLG products, and is even more extreme because Hubsell has tested very little paid traffic.
- At ARPU ≈ $10, the CAC ceiling (≈ $42) is close to a single B2B lead on TikTok/Meta in the US ($25–60). Paid can only be a supplementary channel. The main channels should be organic / referral / partners.

### Gaps
- No OpenView 2024–2026 data (the fund has closed). I found no channel-mix data for Southeast Asia / Vietnam.

## 6. Learning phase: minimum sample and budget, and what this means for 200k VND/day

### Takeaway
- **TikTok official:** volatility "starts to decline after about 25 campaign results or 7 days". The recommended budget is 50x CPA for shallow / high-funnel events and 10x CPA for deep conversions, with a floor of $100–200.
- **Meta:** about 50 optimization events per ad set in 7 days. This figure is cited consistently by third parties; I did not read Meta's own help page this session.
- At 200k VND/day (≈ $7.7), with a CPA around 50–200k VND, the campaign will almost certainly never exit learning. The data is too thin to judge the channel.

### Cited Findings
- **TikTok Help Center, Learning phase:** volatility "starts to decline after about 25 campaign results or 7 days". No fixed conversion count is stated. Pausing, significant edits, or "unreasonable budget setting" can prevent the campaign from exiting learning. — [TikTok Ads – Learning phase](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/learning-phase)
- **TikTok Help Center, Budget best practices:**

  | Event type | Minimum budget | Budget multiple |
  |---|---|---|
  | High-funnel / shallow (e.g. Page View) | 100 USD | 50x current CPA/CPR |
  | Install | 200 USD | 20x CPA |
  | Deep conversion (Add to Cart, Purchase) | 200 USD | 10x CPA |

  - No separate row for Lead.
  - Other rules: do not change targeting / bid / budget before exiting learning; wait at least 2 days between changes; after exiting, keep budget changes within ±50%.
  - [TikTok Ads – Budget best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget-best-practices)
- **Other TikTok sources:**
  - The Search Ads guide recommends a starting daily budget ≈ 20x target CPA. — [TikTok – Search ads optimization](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/search-ads-optimization)
  - The "50 conversions per week" threshold for oCPM is widely cited, but only by third parties such as Nestscale, not on a TikTok page. — [Nestscale](https://support.nestscale.com/nestads/campaign-optimization-checklist-troubleshooting/)
- **Meta (third-party sources):**
  - About 50 optimization events per ad set in 7 days after the last significant edit.
  - Suggested formula: minimum daily budget ≈ (target CPA × 50) / 7.
  - "Learning Limited" means Meta predicts the ad set won't reach 50 events.
  - A budget increase above 20% counts as a significant edit; whether that is Meta's own rule or practitioner experience is disputed.
  - Sources: WebSearch summary of [PPC Land – learning phase](https://ppc.land/learning-phase/), [Jetfuel 2026](https://jetfuel.agency/what-is-the-meta-ads-learning-phase-how-to-exit-it-faster-in-2026/), [Lebesgue 2025](https://lebesgue.io/blog/facebook-ads-learning-phase/).

### Inferences (my own calculations)
- **Lead campaign at 200k VND/lead:**

  | Target | Budget needed |
  |---|---|
  | TikTok's "25 results / 7 days" | 25 × 200k = 5M VND/week ≈ 714k VND/day |
  | "50 conversions / week" (TikTok third parties, Meta) | 10M VND/week ≈ 1.43M VND/day |
  | Meta formula (CPA × 50 / 7) | ≈ 1.43M VND/day |
  | TikTok rule of 10x CPA for deep conversions | ≈ 2M VND/day |

  - **The current 200k VND/day = 1 lead/day = 7 results/week.** That is less than a third of the TikTok threshold and a seventh of the 50/week threshold.
- **Conversation campaign at 50k VND/conversation:** 25 conversations/week costs 1.25M VND/week ≈ 180k VND/day. That is roughly the current budget. So 200k VND/day only makes sense if the campaign optimizes for "conversation" (the shallower event), not "lead".
  - The flip side: the algorithm then optimizes for people who chat, not people who sign up and pay.
- **Minimum test budget to draw any conclusion:**
  - With the goal of ≥30 leads to estimate lead→trial→paid, the cost is about 6M VND per creative/audience variant at 200k/lead.
  - 30 leads × 6% to paid ≈ 1.8 customers. Even 30 leads cannot measure CAC precisely. Measuring CAC with ±50% error needs about 10 customers ≈ 170 leads ≈ 34M VND.
  - This is consistent with the conclusion that paid ads are hard to measure at this scale when ARPU ≈ $10.
- **The ±50% rule** (TikTok: keep budget within the 50% range after exiting learning) means scaling should be gradual. Don't stop and restart: per TikTok's help page, pausing resets learning.

### Gaps
- I did not read Meta's Help Center directly (help pages need login / are blocked), so the "50 events / 7 days" figure comes from consistent third-party sources.
- TikTok publishes no official threshold specifically for the Lead / Instant Messaging objective (the budget page has no "Lead" row).
