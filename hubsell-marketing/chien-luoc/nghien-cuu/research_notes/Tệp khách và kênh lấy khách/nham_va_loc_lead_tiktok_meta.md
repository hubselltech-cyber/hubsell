# Targeting seller-owners and qualifying leads in TikTok / Meta lead gen (for Hubsell, B2B SaaS for TikTok Shop / Shopee sellers)

Research date: 12/11/2026. Sources are mostly TikTok / Meta help centers (dated where the page shows a date) plus some third-party blogs (labelled). Hubsell's situation: ideal buyer spends at least 10M VND/month on GMV Max. Test 1 (Traffic → landing page in the in-app browser) gave 0 signups. Test 2 (Lead gen → TikTok DM, hashtag interactions #gmvmax/#gmv/#banhangtiktokshop, 200k VND/day) gave 1 lead, and the people who wrote in were the wrong audience (an affiliate and a beginner).

## 1. TikTok targeting options for business owners, and TikTok's own lead-gen guidance

### Takeaway
TikTok has no "business owner / ad spender" attribute. Its behaviour targeting (hashtag, video and creator interactions) uses short lookback windows and picks up people who watch content about a topic, which for #gmvmax mostly means learners, affiliates and course sellers. TikTok's own lead-gen guidance is to start broad and spread spend over 3–5 ad groups, with each ad group's daily budget at least 10× the target CPA. Hubsell's 200k VND/day budget cannot meet that rule for a B2B lead. The highest-leverage first-party options are the **Business Account audience** (organic followers, profile visitors, organic video viewers) and **engagement / lead-gen audiences**, plus lookalikes built from them. None of these needs a pixel or a customer-list upload.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok behaviour targeting has three groups: video interactions, creator interactions and hashtag interactions. Third-party guides give short lookback windows: about 7–15 days for video interactions, and up to 90 days for creator follows and profile visits. TikTok's own 2024 targeting deck says "past 30 days" for creator follows, so the sources disagree. — [megadigital.ai (third party)](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ads-behavior-targeting/); [TikTok Targeting Solutions 2024 (PDF)](https://www.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Targeting_Solutions_2024.pdf)
- Hashtag interactions target "people who watched videos with specific hashtags" and suit niche communities. — [Stackmatix (third party)](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-options-guide)
- TikTok's lead-gen best practices (help page, last updated January 2026): launch 3–5 ad groups with different targeting and creative; "start with broadly defined audiences, then narrow"; set each ad group's daily budget to at least 10× the expected CPA; connect Instant Form leads to a CRM. — [TikTok: About the Lead Generation objective](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/lead-generation-objective?lang=en)
- TikTok's AUNZ lead-gen playbook (2024) suggests capping forms at about 6 questions when the goal is lead volume. — [TikTok AUNZ Lead Finders Playbook (PDF)](https://www.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_AUNZ_Lead_Finders_Playbook_Final.pdf) (as summarised in search results; the PDF was not opened)
- TikTok published a 25-page lead-gen best-practice guide with HubSpot covering forms, hooks, messaging and CTAs. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/tiktok-loead-generation-guide-438678)
- **Business Account Custom Audience** (help page, last updated July 2026). Events: Follow (no lookback), 2s / 6s / 100% video view, engagement (like / share / comment), and Visit Profile, each with a 7, 14 or 30-day window. It reaches "both paid and organic followers". Requires your own Business Account; Creator Accounts and Spark Ads are not supported. Can be shared across ad accounts in the same Business Center. — [TikTok: About Business Account Custom Audience](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/business-account-audience?lang=en)
- **Engagement audiences** (help page, last updated July 2025). Organic public videos: 2s / 6s / 100% view and engagement, 7/14/30 days. LIVE: views and engagement, 7/14/30 days. Video ads: click, impression, 2s/6s view, 25/50/75/100% view, up to 180 days. Minimum of 1,000 people to target a custom audience in an ad group. **Lookalikes can be built from an engagement custom audience.** The TikTok account must be linked to Ads Manager before organic or LIVE audiences can be created. — [TikTok: How to create engagement audiences](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/engagement?lang=en)
- **Lead generation Custom Audience**: people who viewed or submitted an Instant Form. Can combine engagement types (e.g. "viewed but not submitted") and exclude. Minimum 1,000 to target; up to 400 custom audiences per account; TikTok placement only. — [TikTok: How to create a lead generation Custom Audience](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/lead-generation-audience?lang=en)
- TikTok's "Generate leads" page suggests that optimising for video views first can raise interest and lower the barrier for a later lead-gen campaign. — [TikTok Business: Generate leads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-SG/goals/generate-leads) (as summarised in search results)
- Jon Loomer (2024) says the video-ad engagement audiences cover ad engagement only, not organic engagement. TikTok's current help pages do offer separate organic sources, so this conflicts with the newer official docs. — [Jon Loomer: TikTok Custom Audiences](https://www.jonloomer.com/introduction-to-tiktok-ads-custom-audiences/); contradicted by [TikTok engagement help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/engagement?lang=en)

### Inferences
- #gmvmax / #gmv / #banhangtiktokshop interactions select for people who **consume GMV Max content**: learners, affiliates and course buyers. Established sellers who spend 10M+/month mostly run ads rather than watch tutorials. This explains the affiliate + beginner result, and adding more hashtags of the same kind will not fix it.
- With 200k VND/day per ad group, the "budget ≥ 10× CPA" rule implies a CPA target of about 20k VND, which is unrealistic for a B2B lead. Delivery will likely stay in learning and chase the cheapest responders. Two options follow: (a) put spend into a single ad group rather than several, and/or (b) use a small, warm retargeting audience where cheap responders are also relevant people.
- Business Account, engagement and lead-gen audiences are TikTok's in-platform data. They need no pixel on hubsell.tech and no customer-list upload, so they fit the "no third-party trackers" policy better than a pixel or a list upload. Whether the policy text covers in-platform retargeting at all still needs to be checked against its exact wording. See memory rule `feedback-doc-chinh-sach-bao-mat-truoc-khi-gan-tracker`.
- A lookalike built from "6s+ viewers of Hubsell's organic demo videos" or "profile visitors" is a reasonable prospecting seed. It meets the 1,000 minimum only if the organic videos have enough 6s viewers in a 30-day window.

### Gaps
- No TikTok interest category such as "Business services / E-commerce sellers" was confirmed for Vietnam, and I could not see the Vietnam interest taxonomy without an Ads Manager login.
- No official, current lookback windows for hashtag interactions; the sources disagree.
- The meaning of "Spark Ads not supported" for the Business Account audience (engagement from Spark-boosted posts not counted, or audience unusable in Spark ads?) is ambiguous and should be checked in Ads Manager.
- Whether lookalikes are available for Vietnam advertisers, and their minimum seed size, was not confirmed.

## 2. Meta equivalents: targeting business owners, Lead Ads forms, Click-to-Messenger qualification

### Takeaway
Meta removed "Small Business Owners" and "Business Owner" as detailed-targeting interests in 2022. The "Facebook Page admins" behaviour reportedly still exists but is broad. Advantage+ Audience is now the default, and it treats detailed targeting only as a suggestion. Meta's strongest qualification tools are form-level: Higher Intent Instant Forms with a review screen, conditional logic that can end the form for unqualified answers, and Messenger Lead Ads, whose template marks which answers count as qualified or disqualified.

### Cited Findings
- Meta removed thousands of detailed-targeting options (announced in late 2021, removed 19/01/2022 and 17/03/2022), including the interests "Small Business Owners" and "Business Owner". — [Reshift Media](https://www.reshiftmedia.com/facebook-removed-detailed-interest-targeting-options/); [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/facebook-removed-thousands-of-detailed-targeting-options/); [Meta developers blog, 08/12/2021](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2021/12/08/updating-metas-detailed-targeting-options/?locale=it_IT)
- Third-party guides describe "Facebook Page admins" (Behaviours > Digital activities) as still available but broad ("from small business owners to teenagers running fan pages"). Its 2025–2026 availability was not confirmed on Meta's own docs. — [EngageWeb](https://www.engageweb.co.uk/blog/two-facebook-ad-targeting-options-youll-want-to-know); [Linear Design (2026 guide)](https://lineardesign.com/blog/facebook-detailed-targeting-list-2024/)
- Advantage+ Audience is the default for most objectives, and in that mode detailed targeting settings act only as suggestions. — [Cropink (third party, Dec 2025)](https://cropink.com/meta-ads-targeting-options)
- Meta has also removed detailed-targeting **exclusions**. Meta says that in its testing, median cost per conversion improved by 22.6% after exclusions were removed. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-removes-more-detailed-ad-targeting-options-facebook-instagram/757856/)
- Higher Intent Instant Forms add a review screen before submitting and run only on mobile Facebook / Instagram Feed. Both form types support conditional logic, up to 15 custom questions, and ending the form based on an answer (for example, ending it for a company below a revenue threshold). These details come from third-party guides; I found no official Meta help page. — [LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/meta-lead-ads-higher-quality-leads/); [GrowthSpree: Meta Lead Ads for B2B SaaS 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-lead-ads-b2b-saas-2026.md); [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-capture-more-qualified-leads-with-facebook-ads/)
- **Messenger Lead Ads** (official developer docs). When someone clicks the ad, Meta's Leadgen app takes control of the thread and asks the questions set in the ad's Message Template. The template defines which answers make a lead complete and which make it disqualified. Disqualified or abandoned flows end with `messenger_lead_gen_incomplete`, which lets your own app run a fallback path for non-leads. Your app can then message within the messaging window (up to 7 days) and read the Q&A through the Conversations API. — [Meta for Developers: Messenger Lead Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/conversation-routing/messenger-lead-ads)
- A Meta study (2023, 15 advertiser-reported 3-cell A/B tests run Nov 2022–Feb 2023) found Instant Forms gave a 20% lower cost per *qualified* lead than similar website forms. This is Meta's own data. — [Meta news (mirror)](https://projecter.workplace.com/business/news/create-more-valuable-leads-than-ever-with-metas-instant-forms); summarised via [MarTech](https://martech.org/lead-gen-grows-on-social-media)
- An independent test by Jon Loomer: Instant Forms gave 114 "quality" leads out of 392 ($7.03 per quality lead), against 69 out of 238 ($13.01) for website forms. — [Jon Loomer: Testing quality leads](https://www.jonloomer.com/testing-quality-leads/)

### Inferences
- On Meta, too, targeting cannot isolate "sellers spending 10M+/month on GMV Max". Filtering has to come from creative and form or chat qualification. Messenger Lead Ads are the closest Meta counterpart to TikTok's DM automated chat, with the bonus of a built-in "disqualified" outcome.
- Hubsell's Facebook fanpage is small (memory: 1 follower as of 03/11), so Page-engagement retargeting on Meta has almost no seed today.

### Gaps
- Vietnam availability of Higher Intent forms, conditional logic and Messenger Lead Ads was not confirmed. These features are generally global, but I did not verify this.
- No official Meta help-centre page was retrieved for Instant Form question limits.
- Click-to-WhatsApp is probably low-relevance in Vietnam, where Zalo dominates, but I did not verify market-share figures.

## 3. Lead qualification in DMs and forms: questions, friction, auto-replies, routing; the volume vs quality trade-off

### Takeaway
TikTok now offers real qualification tools in Vietnam. These include Instant Form qualifying questions (Preferred / Not Preferred leads with different end pages), logic with an **Exit form** action, the DM welcome-message template with suggested questions, automated chat with branching, and Message Assistant (an AI DM bot that supports Vietnamese). The trap is TikTok's DM **"Leads" goal**: it counts any chat that contains a phone number or email as a lead, so the algorithm learns to find people who readily type a phone number, not people who spend 10M+ on ads. Every source agrees that adding friction lowers volume and raises cost per lead. No source gives TikTok-specific numbers on what happens to cost per *qualified* lead.

### Cited Findings
- **Instant Form question types** (help page, last updated August 2026): Short Answer, Multiple Choice (up to 10 options), Appointment Scheduling, Image Select (up to 8), Conditional Answer (up to 5, via CSV), and Business Locator (not available to all advertisers). Personal-info questions are prefilled from the TikTok profile "whenever possible". — [TikTok: About Instant Form question types and settings](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-instant-form-question-types-and-settings?lang=en)
- **Qualifying Question**: one Multiple Choice (or Image Select) question can be set as the qualifier. It sorts leads into **Preferred / Not Preferred** and can send each group to a different Final Page. **Logic Settings** offers "Go to next question / Complete all custom questions / **Exit form**"; Exit form ends the form without collecting the person's info, and TikTok warns it "may increase your cost per lead". Logic Settings is not available to all advertisers. — same source
- **Card layout**: logic is available when a form has more than 2 custom questions. — [TikTok: About the Card layout for Instant Forms](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-the-question-card-form)
- **DM welcome-message template**: a required greeting (200 characters), optional suggested questions, and an optional form card (one per template). Form-card leads sync to Leads Center and any connected CRM. Templates are reviewed before going live. Suggested questions disappear after the user taps one. — [TikTok: About the welcome message template](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-welcome-message-template-for-tiktok-direct-messaging-ads); [How to set up the welcome message template](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-set-up-a-welcome-message-template-for-tiktok-direct-messaging-ads)
- **Automated chat for DM ads** (last updated October 2026): structured, scripted conversations "to capture more qualified leads". You enter custom answers and set where each answer leads (branching). It is set up by default when the campaign goal is the Leads goal. TikTok's tips: open warmly, keep questions short, ask for contact details at the end. It may not be available to all accounts. — [TikTok: How to set up automated chat](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-set-up-automated-chat-for-tiktok-direct-messaging-ads?lang=en)
- **The "Leads" optimization goal for DM ads is available only in Southeast Asia**. **Auto lead capture**, which it requires, is limited to Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand and **Vietnam**. Turning it on requires uploading a privacy policy and company details for review. The system "scans chat replies for common formats such as phone numbers and emails" and does not read full conversations. Attribution is last touch; the German version of the page gives 7-day click / 1-day view. — [TikTok: About the Lead optimization goal](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-lead-optimization-goal); [How to use auto lead capture](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-use-smart-lead-creation-for-business-accounts)
- **Message Assistant** (formerly Lead Genie; help page May 2026) is an AI chatbot for Verified Business Accounts in TikTok DMs. It answers from a Knowledge Base and gathers contact info into Leads Center. It is in testing with limited accounts in countries that include **Vietnam**, and it **supports Vietnamese**. It hands over to a human on request. — [TikTok: About Message Assistant](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-tiktok-lead-genie)
- TikTok internal data (2024): replying within 3 minutes can raise conversation engagement rate by up to 2.2×. — [TikTok blog: Messaging Ads, 03/06/2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/messaging-ads)
- **Volume vs quality evidence.** In a Meta-published case, a longer form gave higher-quality leads while a shorter form gave more leads at a lower cost per lead; cost per qualified lead was not reported. — summarised in search results via [Madgicx](https://madgicx.com/blog/facebook-lead-ads-examples). Agencies widely claim that Higher Intent and qualifying questions raise CPL but lower cost per qualified lead, but I found no independent matched test with published figures. — [LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/meta-lead-ads-higher-quality-leads/); [Directive](https://directiveconsulting.com/blog/facebook-lead-generation-ads-quality-guide/)
- A question that cannot be prefilled (for example, a monthly ad-budget band) forces genuine thought and filters out low-intent submissions. Third parties also caution that finance-related questions can run into Meta's personal-attributes policy. — [LeadSync (third party)](https://leadsync.me/blog/qualify-leads-facebook-lead-ads/)

### Inferences
- The 1 lead / 4 conversations result fits how the Leads goal works: the model optimises toward "types a phone number in DM", which beginners and affiliates who want help do readily. A better setup is a welcome template whose **first suggested question is the qualifier**, for example tap options such as "Chi quảng cáo GMV Max/tháng: <5tr / 5–10tr / 10–30tr / >30tr", with the branches of the automated chat routing low bands to a free guide and high bands to a request for phone/Zalo. Ad-spend band and orders/day are better qualifiers than "do you sell on TikTok?".
- On an Instant Form, the equivalent is a multiple-choice ad-spend question set as the Qualifying Question, with Not Preferred sent to a different final page (self-serve signup link) rather than Exit form. That keeps the cost signal for the algorithm while separating the leads. Exit form is the harsher option, and TikTok itself warns it raises CPL.
- Hubsell's TikTok account was verified as a business on 10/11 (memory), which is a prerequisite for Message Assistant. Its Knowledge Base could pre-answer price and feature questions and ask the spend-band question.
- Turning on auto lead capture requires uploading the privacy policy. Check that the policy text allows TikTok to collect contact info from chat on Hubsell's behalf.

### Gaps
- No TikTok numbers on cost per qualified lead with and without qualifying questions or Exit form.
- Whether automated chat, Logic Settings and Message Assistant are actually enabled on Hubsell's ad account can only be seen in Ads Manager, since all are "may not be available".
- No source explains whether TikTok's optimisation uses the Preferred / Not Preferred split as a signal; it is probably reporting-only, but this is unverified.

## 4. Creative and messages that make established sellers self-select (vs beginners); B2B / merchant case studies on TikTok

### Takeaway
B2B paid-social practitioners agree that the creative is the main filter on platforms with broad and weak targeting. Name the role, state the threshold, say who it is *not* for, and accept a lower CTR. The closest official TikTok case for recruiting merchants is Lazada, which used the Lead Generation objective with an Instant Form to "capture and qualify prospective merchants": 47% of submitters became active sellers within a week. I found no published TikTok case of a SaaS sold to marketplace sellers in Vietnam.

### Cited Findings
- "If your ad could appeal to anyone, it will attract everyone." Ask who the ad *repels*; if the answer is no one, the algorithm will find the cheapest, lowest-intent clicks. — [GrowthSpree: Ad creative that filters (B2B SaaS)](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/b2b-saas-ad-creative-that-filters) (third party)
- Explicit ICP pain points and exclusionary language let prospects qualify themselves before clicking, trading volume for pipeline quality. — [SaaS Hero (2026)](https://www.saashero.net/strategy/b2b-saas-ads-lead-quality/)
- When targeting is broad, the creative should speak to the desired audience in language that discourages beginners from clicking; expect a low CTR. — [Search Engine Land: Improve PPC lead quality for B2B](https://searchengineland.com/improve-ppc-lead-quality-b2b-campaigns-448840)
- Qualifier copy templates: "If you're a {ROLE} at a {COMPANY_TYPE}…", "Not for agencies", "See if you qualify". If clicks look fine but qualification is weak, change the audience or the qualifying copy first. — [Atticus Li: X/Twitter ads for B2B SaaS](https://atticusli.com/blog/posts/twitter-ads-experiments-for-b2b-saas-audience-stacks-and-hook-copy-that-fill-demo-calendars/) (third party)
- Cost per lead can fall while cost per qualified opportunity rises; manage against cost per qualified lead. — [SaaS Hero](https://saashero.net/google-ppc/b2b-saas-ads-prequalify-leads/)
- **Lazada x TikTok** (Southeast Asia; follow-on always-on campaign in Thailand): Lead Generation objective + Instant Form with basic details prefilled, "to capture and qualify prospective merchants"; native TikTok-style videos; targeting left to TikTok's recommendation system; 47% of submitters became active sellers within a week. — [TikTok case study (original)](https://www.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/lazada-295); summary at [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/tiktok-for-business/case-study/finding-new-customers-and-growing-its-seller-base-with-a-powerful-lead-generation-campaign)
- Vietnamese and SEA messaging-ads cases from TikTok are all B2C (beauty, dental, travel), not merchant SaaS. One example is Linh Anh Beauty Clinic (VN): "lower CPA and CPM and higher qualified lead conversion" with no figures given. — [TikTok blog: Messaging Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/messaging-ads); [Tugo Travel case](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/tugo-travel-agency-case-study)
- HubSpot research cited secondhand says TikTok delivers 1.8× the share of conversions relative to its share of spend. Other guides treat TikTok as mainly a top-of-funnel channel for B2B. — [Single Grain (secondhand)](https://www.singlegrain.com/lead-generation/how-b2b-tiktok-lead-generation-drives-1-8x-better-results/)

### Inferences
- For Hubsell, a filtering hook could be: "Shop đang chi trên 10 triệu/tháng cho GMV Max? …" ("Shop spending over 10M/month on GMV Max? …"), plus a real Ads Manager screen with spend and ROI columns at that scale, plus "Không dành cho người mới bắt đầu / affiliate" ("Not for beginners / affiliates"). This uses the same mechanism the sources describe; it is my inference, not tested.
- Showing a real dashboard with spend and ROI numbers at the 10M–100M VND level gives a natural filter, because beginners cannot relate to it. Hubsell's memory rule forbids showing shop names or customer data, so use demo or anonymised numbers.
- The Lazada case shows Instant Form plus prefill works for merchant acquisition in SEA. But Lazada's offer (free seller signup) is much lower-commitment than a paid SaaS, so a 47% conversion rate should not be expected.

### Gaps
- No public TikTok or Meta case studies found for Shopify, Square, Toast or SEA merchant SaaS (e.g. Sapo, Haravan, KiotViet) lead gen with numbers.
- No A/B data on "spend call-out" hooks versus generic hooks on TikTok.

## 5. Retargeting organic video viewers (engagement custom audience) to monetise organic reach

### Takeaway
This is officially supported and needs no pixel. Link the Business Account to Ads Manager, then build audiences of organic-video viewers (2s / 6s / 100%), engagers, profile visitors or followers with up to a 30-day lookback; followers have no lookback. Retargeting needs at least 1,000 people, and lookalikes can be built from these audiences. For Hubsell, whose best organic video brought about 100 natural signups, this is the cheapest way to show lead ads to people who already self-selected by watching a seller-specific demo.

### Cited Findings
- Organic public-video audiences: 2s, 6s and 100% view and engagement, with 7/14/30-day windows; LIVE: views and engagement, 7/14/30 days. Requires the TikTok account to be linked to Ads Manager. A lookalike can be made from an engagement audience. Minimum 1,000 to target. — [TikTok: engagement audiences](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/engagement?lang=en)
- Business Account audience: follows (no lookback), video views, engagement and profile visits (7/14/30 days); reaches organic and paid followers; Creator Accounts and Spark Ads are not supported. — [TikTok: Business Account audience](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/business-account-audience?lang=en)
- Lead-gen audience: "viewed form but did not submit" can be retargeted; minimum 1,000. — [TikTok: lead generation audience](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/lead-generation-audience?lang=en)
- Video ads' engagement windows go up to 180 days, longer than organic (30). — [TikTok: engagement audiences](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/engagement?lang=en)

### Inferences
- A suggested structure: Audience A = 6s+ viewers and profile visitors of Hubsell's organic demo videos (30 days), plus followers. Run a DM or Instant Form lead ad with the spend-band qualifier to Audience A, and exclude existing customers if a TikTok-side list is possible (not a customer-list upload; see the privacy note). Audience B = a lookalike of A, for prospecting instead of hashtag interactions.
- The 30-day organic window means the audience decays quickly. It needs a steady flow of organic seller-focused videos to stay above 1,000.
- With a small audience, frequency will be high at 200k VND/day. Rotate several creatives.
- Who watched the organic video still matters: if the organic video reached beginners, its viewer audience inherits that mix. The qualifier question remains necessary.

### Gaps
- Whether Hubsell's organic video viewers reach the 1,000 minimum within 30 days is unknown; check the audience size in Ads Manager.
- Confirm that Hubsell's posting account is a Business Account (not a Creator account) and is linked to the advertiser.

## 6. Does the TikTok in-app browser hurt SaaS signups? Best practices

### Takeaway
Yes, mechanically. Since 30/09/2021 Google has blocked OAuth sign-in in embedded webviews, which returns "403: disallowed_useragent" in TikTok, Instagram and Facebook in-app browsers. A landing page whose main CTA is "Sign in with Google" therefore breaks for in-app traffic. I found no published benchmark for how large the drop is. The fixes are: detect the in-app user agent and offer a non-Google path (phone or Zalo OTP, email); prompt users to open the page in Safari or Chrome; or avoid the landing page entirely by capturing phone or Zalo in a TikTok Instant Form, DM, or Zalo Instant Messaging Ad.

### Cited Findings
- Google blocks OAuth requests from embedded webviews; enforcement began 30/09/2021. — [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/upcoming-security-changes-to-googles-oauth-20-authorization-endpoint-in-embedded-webviews/)
- Users report "403: disallowed_useragent" from Instagram and TikTok embedded browsers. — [Auth0 Community](https://community.auth0.com/t/google-social-error-for-web-login-from-embedded-browsers/110494)
- Suggested fixes: detect the webview user agent and prompt to open in Safari or Chrome; use SFSafariViewController or Chrome Custom Tabs in native apps; offer non-Google sign-in. — [link.boo: Google login fails in TikTok browser](https://link.boo/fix/google-login-fails-in-tiktok-browser) (vendor); [Terra docs](https://docs.tryterra.co/faq/help-topics/connecting/oauth-webview-and-sign-in-failures/oauth-webview-blocked-use-native-browser)
- TikTok Instant Forms load natively in the app and prefill personal info from the profile "whenever possible". — [TikTok: Generate leads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-SG/goals/generate-leads); [TikTok: Instant Form question types](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-instant-form-question-types-and-settings?lang=en)
- **Zalo Instant Messaging Ads exist in Vietnam.** Setup: a Zalo Official Account linked to TikTok Ads Manager; objective Lead generation, then optimisation location "Instant messaging apps". Goals are Clicks or Conversations (Conversations recommended). Manual campaigns are not supported for Zalo under the Conversations goal, which means Smart+ only. Zalo postbacks follow Zalo's own attribution windows. — [TikTok: Zalo direct integration for Instant Messaging Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-set-up-direct-integration-with-zalo-for-instant-messaging-ads); [TikTok: How to set up Instant Messaging Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-set-up-tiktok-instant-messaging-ads?lang=en)
- TikTok Messaging Ads (June 2025): Direct Messaging Ads keep users in TikTok, and Instant Messaging Ads send them to Messenger, WhatsApp, **Zalo** or LINE. Smart+ is recommended as the default. ### Inferences
- Test 1 (423 clicks, 0 signups) is consistent with the Google-OAuth block plus a long form in the webview. It is not proof that the audience was wrong, but test 2 suggests the audience was also off. Both problems need fixing.
- For a SaaS that requires connecting shops through OAuth with Shopee or TikTok anyway, the realistic paid-social funnel is: capture phone or Zalo inside TikTok (Instant Form or DM qualifier), then follow up by Zalo or phone, and the person signs up later on desktop or in the mobile app. This is my inference, consistent with how the TikTok tools are designed.
- Zalo IM Ads move the conversation to Zalo, where Vietnamese sellers already work. They require Smart+, which conflicts with tight manual targeting, so qualification would have to rely on the creative and the first Zalo OA auto-reply.
