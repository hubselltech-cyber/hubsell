# Thư trả lời Apple lần 4 — 2.1(b) Information Needed (11/10/2026)

Bối cảnh: lần nộp 4 (submission `6b88b07c…`, build 1.0 (8) + 20 IAP, nộp 10/10 11:27) được
Apple xem 11/10 trên iPhone 17 Pro Max và trả **Guideline 2.1(b) - Information Needed**: họ hỏi
5 câu về mô hình kinh doanh trước khi xem tiếp. KHÔNG bắt lỗi 3.1.1 nữa; 20 IAP vẫn "Ready for
Review". Dán thư dưới vào **Reply to App Review** của submission đó. Không cần build mới.

Nguyên tắc khi trả lời: nói thật, nhất quán với thư lần 3. Có IAP rồi nên dựa vào 3.1.3(b)
(đa nền tảng), KHÔNG xin ngoại lệ 3.1.3(c) Enterprise. Câu 5 trả lời đúng sự thật: bán cho
người bán hàng/doanh nghiệp, không phải người tiêu dùng hay gia đình.

```
Hello,

Thank you for reviewing Hubsell. Here are the answers to your questions.

1. Who are the users that will use the paid content in the app?
Online merchants in Vietnam - companies and household businesses that sell on
e-commerce marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop). The shop owner and the
staff accounts the owner creates (for example warehouse staff) use the app to
manage orders, inventory, finance reports and advertising for their own shop.
Hubsell is not a consumer app; it has no content for end consumers.

2. Where can users purchase the content that can be accessed in the app?
- Inside the iOS app with In-App Purchase: Settings ("Cau hinh") -> "Goi dich vu".
  20 non-renewing subscriptions (Starter / Growth / Pro / Business / Scale for
  1 / 3 / 6 / 12 months) are submitted with this version.
- On our website (web app for desktop), where the same plans are sold to
  merchants who use Hubsell on the web.
The app has no link, button or text that points to purchasing outside the app.

3. What specific types of previously purchased content can a user access in the app?
Only the merchant's active Hubsell service plan. The plan determines limits of
the business tools on the shop account (number of connected marketplace shops,
staff accounts, order volume and features such as finance reports and the
advertising assistant). There is no media, digital goods or consumable content.
A merchant who bought a plan on the website signs in and keeps using the same
plan in the app, as allowed by Guideline 3.1.3(b), since the same plans are
available via In-App Purchase.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?
None are sold or unlocked inside the app without In-App Purchase. The only
features available without an in-app purchase are those of a plan the merchant
already bought on our website (question 3).

5. Are the enterprise services in your app sold to single users, consumers, or for family use?
No. Plans are sold to businesses: each plan belongs to one merchant shop
account (the business) and covers the owner plus the staff accounts of that
shop. We do not sell to consumers or for family use.

Demo account (Business plan) is in App Review Information. To test the purchase:
Settings -> "Goi dich vu" -> choose a plan and period -> "Mua goi".

Thank you,
Hubsell Technology Co., Ltd.
```
